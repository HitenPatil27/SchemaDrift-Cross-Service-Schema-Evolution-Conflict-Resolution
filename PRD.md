# 17. SchemaDrift — Cross-Service Schema Evolution Conflict Resolution

**17. SchemaDrift — Cross-Service Schema Evolution Conflict Resolution**
**Problem Statement**
Schema drift is dangerous when services remain syntactically healthy while interpreting the same data differently, especially during a rolling deployment in which old and new consumers coexist. Design a simulated exchange among independently versioned services whose schemas carry machine-readable compatibility contracts for required fields, representations, and supported transformations. Inject a producer-side change while both old and new consumers are active. The system must identify which consumers remain compatible, detect a subtle case that is structurally valid but semantically incompatible under the declared contracts, and prevent newly incompatible records from silently flowing downstream. It must also provide a bounded correction path for records already produced during the incompatible window, such as re-transforming or quarantining only the affected records, while leaving compatible traffic untouched. A convincing demonstration should include one safe evolution, one obvious break, and one silent reinterpretation case, then verify that the affected consumer and records are correctly identified, protected from further bad data, and brought back to a contract-valid state.
